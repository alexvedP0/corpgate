Ruchit Desai, Partner

Ruchit is a trailblazing entrepreneur with a keen eye for innovation and excellence. After pursuing his degree from the renowned NM College of Commerce, Ruchit embarked on an impressive entrepreneurial journey, establishing multiple successful ventures.


Ruchit founded Purec Hygiene, which revolutionized the market by introducing the world’s first automatic seat cover. Purec Hygiene manufactures smart washroom essentials for those committed to a hygienic lifestyle.


Building on this success, he launched CORPGATE, a company dedicated to streamlining business procurement processes, and enhancing their efficiency and effectiveness. As a founder and serial entrepreneur, Ruchit handles marketing, sales, and client relations, ensuring these companies' growth and smooth functioning.


His unwavering commitment to excellence has established him as an excellent leader in the industry, continually driving success and setting new benchmarks in all the ventures he undertakes.
